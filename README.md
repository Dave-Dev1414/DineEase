# DineEase

A smart restaurant platform for discovering, reserving, ordering, and reducing food waste.

## About

DineEase is a restaurant platform designed to connect customers, restaurants, and approved food-rescue organizations in one system.
Customers can discover restaurants, make reservations, order food, and track their orders.
Restaurants can manage their menus, reservations, orders, customers, and surplus food while recovering value from food that would otherwise go to waste.

## Core Features

- Restaurant discovery and search
- Restaurant profiles and menus
- Table reservations
- Online food ordering
- Order tracking
- Customer accounts
- Restaurant management dashboard
- Surplus food management
- Discounted surplus listings
- Food donation management
- Expiry tracking and alerts
- Donation history and reports
- WhatsApp notifications
- AI-powered recommendations and assistance
- Admin management

## User Roles

- Customers
- Restaurant owners and staff
- Approved food-donation organizations
- Administrators
